# Teaching Notes

- Learners with dyslexia or dyscalculia may find spatial-pattern tasks like clock reading especially frustrating. Be encouraging, frame progress as normal.
- Target watches are 2-hand dials with no numerals. Lessons should use clean, minimal dials without numbers.
- Interactive/gamified drills resonate more than passive reading for most learners.
- Goal is practical speed, not perfection. "Close enough" (within ~2 minutes) is a win early on.
