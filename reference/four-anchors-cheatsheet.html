Reading Algorithm

1. Hour hand quadrant → narrows to 3 possible hours
2. Hour hand position within quadrant → picks the exact hour
3. Minute hand quadrant → gives you the quarter
4. Minute hand position within quadrant → refines to ~5 min

Key Rule

Don't count tick marks. Identify the nearest anchor, then estimate the offset. "A bit past :30" is faster and good enough.
