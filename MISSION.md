# Mission: Read Analog Watches Instantly

## Why
People with learning disorders such as dyslexia and dyscalculia often struggle with spatial-pattern tasks like reading analog watch faces — especially minimalist dials without numerals. Existing tools assume the skill is intuitive, leaving these learners without structured practice. This project provides a focused, repetitive trainer that builds the pattern-recognition needed to read a two-hand analog watch at a glance.

## Success looks like
- Glance at watch and know the time within ~1 second, no counting tick marks
- Correctly identify the hour without second-guessing
- Approximate minutes to the nearest 5 without effort
- Feel confident and natural wearing numberless watches

## Constraints
- Two-hand watches only (hour + minute, no second hand)
- No numerals on the dial (may have indices/tick marks)
- Learning should fit into short daily sessions
- Primarily a pattern-recognition/spatial skill, not a knowledge problem

## Out of scope
- Complications (chronographs, moon phase, etc.)
- Watch collecting, history, or mechanics
- Reading watches with numerals (already functional, just slower than desired)
