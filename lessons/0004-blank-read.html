Lesson 4: Read the Blank Watch

No markers. No options. Just you and the hands.

This is the graduation test. You've learned the four anchors, drilled the quadrants, and built the spatial map by setting clocks. Now prove it: read a completely blank watch face and type what time it shows.

No crutches. There are no tick marks, no numbers, and no multiple-choice options to eliminate. You see two hands on a bare circle — trust your spatial sense and type the time.

Your answer needs to be within 5 minutes of the actual time, and the hour must be exact. This is how real analog reading works — you glance and know "it's about ten past three," not "it's 3:11."

What You're Building

When this feels easy — when you glance at the blank dial and the time just appears in your mind — you've built the skill. The watch isn't a puzzle to decode anymore. It's a shape you read, the same way you read a word without sounding out each letter.
